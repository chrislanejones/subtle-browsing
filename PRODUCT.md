# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chrome users browsing articles and other everyday websites who want to stop intrusive video and resource-heavy ads while allowing normal banners. The toolbar is for quick decisions; DevTools is for inspection and the settings page manages saved rules.

## Product Purpose

Keep browsing quiet and responsive without indiscriminately blocking publisher advertising. Preserve intentional video playback and make exceptions and recovery easy.

## Brand Personality

Utilitarian, quiet, direct. These are the existing UI's stated design direction and the README's plain-language voice.

## Anti-references

Blanket ad blocking conflicts with the README's purpose. Decorative dashboards and distracting motion conflict with the existing lightweight utility UI.

## Design Principles

- Keep ordinary ads and intentional content working.
- Make global and site-only scope explicit.
- Offer recovery when removing content.
- Reuse familiar controls across toolbar, settings and DevTools.
- Avoid continuous polling and unnecessary page work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve existing labeled native controls, keyboard focus, light/dark themes, forced-color support and reduced-motion styles. No additional user-specific accessibility requirements were supplied.
